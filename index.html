01 PHILOSOPHY

Humans have always thought in patterns. We carve that instinct into wood.

For thousands of years, every culture arrived at the same discovery: geometry holds attention. Temple ceilings, tile work, woven screens — patterns that reward a long look, drawn and redrawn across generations. Evoque Living brings that heritage into modern decor.

Every piece begins as mathematics — a circle divided, a star constructed, a motif repeated with a precision no freehand could reach. Technology cuts it. Hands finish it. The result reads differently from every distance you stand at.

02 THE GEOMETRY

Every piece begins as a proof.

I — THE CIRCLE

One radius, turned about a point. Every mandala that has ever been drawn inherits from this.

II — THE DIVISION

Twelve equal parts. The compass decides where they fall — not the eye.

III — THE STAR

Connect every fifth point and a twelve-fold star locks into place. No line is arbitrary.

IV — THE BLOOM

Arcs meet arcs. The lattice takes on petals — and the proof becomes a pattern.

Q.E.D.

…and the pattern becomes T901, Alpha Tale.

05 THE CRAFT

Drawn by mathematics. Finished by hand.

Digital craftsmanship is not a shortcut — it is how a thousand-cut pattern survives translation into wood without losing a single line.

01
DRAFTED

Each motif is constructed in software as pure geometry — ratios first, ornament second.

02
CUT

A laser follows the drawing to a fraction of a millimetre, sheet by sheet by sheet.

03
LAYERED

Sheets are stacked, aligned and bonded — depth checked against the drawing at every step.

04
FINISHED

Stains and coats are brushed by hand. A 14-point inspection closes every piece.
